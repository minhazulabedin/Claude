WHAT IS DENGUE?
ডেঙ্গু কী?
Dengue is a viral illness spread by the bite of an infected Aedes mosquito. It does not spread by touch, coughs or sharing food.
KNOW THE AEDES
Black, with white stripes on the legs
Bites by day — early morning and late afternoon
Breeds in clean, still water
Egg to adult in 8–10 days
HOW IT SPREADS
1
A mosquito bites a person who has dengue.
2
The virus grows inside the mosquito for about 8–12 days.
3
It bites someone else and passes dengue on.
There is no specific medicine that cures dengue — so prevention and early care matter most.
HIGHER RISK OF SEVERE DENGUE
Babies and young children, older people, pregnant women, people with long-term illness, and anyone who has had dengue before.

DANGER SIGNS:
GO TO HOSPITAL NOW
They often start 1–2 days after the fever goes down.
Severe belly pain
Vomiting 3 or more times in a day
Bleeding gums or nose
Blood in vomit or stool
Very tired, restless or irritable
ANY FEVER IN DENGUE SEASON?
See a doctor and get tested — don't wait for it to pass.
Feeling better when the fever drops does not mean it is over — keep watching for 2 more days.
3
HAVE A FEVER? ACT EARLY.
জ্বর হলে করণীয়
EARLY CARE SAVES LIVES
Most people recover with rest and fluids — and severe dengue can be treated when it is caught early.
Get tested early
Ask a doctor for an NS1 test in the first days of fever, and a blood count (CBC) to check platelets.
Drink plenty
Water, ORS, daab (green coconut water), fruit juice and soup — even when you are not thirsty.
Paracetamol only
No aspirin, ibuprofen or other painkillers — they can cause bleeding. Antibiotics do not treat dengue.
Rest under a net
Sleep under a mosquito net, day and night, so mosquitoes can't carry it to your family.
Keep watching
Check for danger signs every day, especially as the fever goes down.
WATCH FOR DEHYDRATION
Passing less urine, a dry mouth or lips, or few or no tears means too little fluid — see a doctor.
NO STILL WATER · NO AEDES · NO DENGUE
