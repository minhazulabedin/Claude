HAVE A FEVER? ACT EARLY.
Early care saves lives · জ্বর হলে দেরি নয়
Get tested (NS1) in the first days of fever
Rest and drink plenty: water, ORS, daab, soup
Paracetamol only: no aspirin, no ibuprofen
Sleep under a net to stop it spreading
DANGER SIGNS? GO TO HOSPITAL.
They often start as the fever goes down.
Severe belly pain
Vomiting 3 or more times in a day
Bleeding gums or nose
Blood in vomit or stool
Very tired, restless or irritable
