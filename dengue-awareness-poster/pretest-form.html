POSTER PRE-TEST · DENGUE STARTS AT HOME.

Department of Public Health, Independent University, Bangladesh · Group · Date

How to run it: show the A4 draft to 10 people (5 IUB students, 5 local residents or building caretakers). Let each person look for 30 seconds, then take the poster away. Ask the five questions below and tick ✓ in the table when the answer is correct. Write any comments for question 5.

THE FIVE QUESTIONS (with expected answers)

  1. What is this poster about? — Preventing dengue at home / stopping mosquitoes breeding.
  2. Name two places at home where dengue mosquitoes breed. — Any two: rooftop tank or tubs, flower-pot trays, AC drip water, fridge drip tray, stored water drums or buckets, tyres, shells or packets.
  3. How often should stored water be emptied? — Every 3 days.
  4. Name one danger sign that means "go to hospital now". — Any one: severe belly pain, vomiting 3+ times a day, bleeding gums or nose, blood in vomit or stool, very tired, restless or irritable.
  5. Is anything confusing, offensive or hard to read? — Note the answer word for word.
