# From Being Heard to Creating Change: poster and leaflet

World Mental Health Day 2026 poster and A4 tri-fold leaflet for Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB).
Prepared by Group 12 (Health and Society): (G-12)-Shoada, (G-12)-Muna, (G-12)-Mahee, from the group's
own notes of 30-09-26.

- **Title (the group's):** "From Being Heard to Creating Change"
- **Theme line (exact wording, on the ribbon, 36 pt):** "Lived experiences heard: real voices, real change."
- **Slogan (the group's):** "Your voice can start a conversation. Your action can create change."
- **Steps:** Speak → Be heard → Take action → Create change on the poster; Speak → Listen → Understand →
  Act → Create change in the leaflet.
- **Call to action (the group's):** "Join the conversation — be a part of it. Work together and take small,
  meaningful steps."
- **Domain:** Positive Change (dignity, better support, reduced stigma), marked "Our focus" on the poster.

## The poster

The group's title sits at the top in 92 px (about 69 pt, bold), with the IUB logo on the left and the
Department of Public Health and university name on the right. The theme line, written exactly, runs on
the coral ribbon underneath.

The central picture fills about 40% of the sheet: a bridge made of speech bubbles. The people are photo
cut-outs with blurred faces, placed into the drawn scene. The layout follows the group's mockup.

- Left, "Unheard": a grey cliff with signposts reading STIGMA, SILENCE and LEFT OUT. A faded grey person
  walks toward the bridge, and their faint, unanswered speech bubble is where it begins.
- The bridge: the bubbles change from grey "…" to teal voice waves, then coral ticks, then amber sprouts.
  A story is spoken, heard, acted on, and grows into change.
- The top: a speech bubble with voice waves is answered by one with a tick, the moment of being heard.
  There is no romantic imagery, only peers listening.
- Right, "Heard & included": in a sunny park, four friends sit around a café table, one of them in a
  wheelchair. An empty orange chair is pulled out: "A seat at the table". The park is softly out of focus
  so the group stands out.
- The gap: a path winds through a meadow under the bridge, with the group's slogan over it.

The journey strip reads Speak, Be heard, Take action, Create change ("Our focus"), with short captions:
"Share your thoughts and ideas." "Listen and understand other views." "Turn ideas into action, together."
"Small actions bring less stigma and better support." The call to action is the group's, with three
example steps underneath: offer peer support, challenge stigma, check in on a friend.

The bottom rows carry the support points, with no phone numbers at the group's request ("Need support?
Talk to someone you trust · IUB student counselling"), the respect line ("Every story is different: share
with consent, listen with respect.") and the footer with the member names. The full support panel with
16263 and 999 stays inside the leaflet. All six parts from the course sample are present: caption,
picture, key benefits, support points, call for actions, logo.

## The leaflet (A4 tri-fold)

Inside (page 2): the group's five steps, each with its own line from the notes and three things to try.

- Speak: "Don't be afraid to share your ideas and concerns."
- Listen: "Respect other people's opinions and experience."
- Understand: "Hear from different perspectives and identify what needs to change."
- Act: "Work together and take small, meaningful steps."
- Create change: "Small actions can create a better community."

The inside also covers what lived experience means, why being heard matters, "Nothing about us, without
us", and words to say or avoid when listening.

Outside (page 1): the inside flap holds "My small step this week", a tick-box pledge with space for your
own step. The back panel says where to find support (someone you trust, university counselling, Shastho
Batayon 16263, emergency 999), how to share with care, when to check in, and the Group 12 credit with the
department and university names. The front cover carries the title, the bridge scene, the theme line, the
slogan, the five steps and the IUB logo.

Print both pages double-sided, flipped on the short edge, at 100% ("actual size"). To fold, lay the sheet
inside-up, fold the right panel (Act / Create change) in first, then the left panel (Speak) over it. The
cover ends up on the front and the pledge list is the first thing a reader sees on opening.

## Spec compliance (poster)

- A2 portrait, 420 × 594 mm, 300 dpi output
- Title 92 px ≈ 69 pt bold (brief asks 48–72); theme ribbon 48 px = 36 pt and slogan 50 px = 37.5 pt
  (36–48); section heading and step titles 40 px = 30 pt (28–36); all supporting text at least 27 px ≈ 20 pt
- About 140 words; measured 61% visual and 39% text by page area
- Two font families: Fraunces and Work Sans
- Palette: teal `#12626B`, coral `#E0684B`, amber `#E89A3C`, plus ink `#1E2A2B` and ivory `#FAF6EF`
- Every face is blurred, so nobody can be identified. Distress appears only as a faded grey figure walking
  toward the bridge, never as a suffering person.

## About the photo people

The people come from the group's own AI-generated mockup (724 × 1024 px). The cut-outs were denoised,
upscaled 8×, gently sharpened and given fine grain (OpenCV, no AI upscaler), so they meet 300 dpi at
their printed size, and every face was blurred. This cleans the picture up but cannot add detail the
mockup never had, so the people stay slightly soft up close. Everything else on the poster is vector and
prints sharp. For a sharper print, replace the files in `people/` with higher-resolution versions in the
same poses on a transparent background, ideally free-licence stock photos with faces hidden, and credit
them here.

## Before submitting

- Check the helpline numbers in the leaflet (16263, 999) are still current.
- Confirm the exact name and location of the IUB student counselling service.
- Print an A4 test copy of the poster (teal often prints darker) and check it in greyscale.
- Run the pre-test with `pretest-form-A4.pdf` on 10 students. Fix anything fewer than 8 of 10 got right,
  then show the course teacher before the final print.
- Print the leaflet double-sided, fold it to check the panel order, photocopy it, and submit the printed
  leaflet to the course teacher.

## Regenerating the print files

- Poster: open `poster.html` in Chromium at a 1587 × 2245 viewport. Screenshot at deviceScaleFactor ≈ 3.13
  for the 300 dpi PNG. Print to PDF at 420 × 594 mm with backgrounds enabled.
- Leaflet: open `leaflet.html` at a 1123 px wide viewport. Screenshot each `.sheet` at deviceScaleFactor
  3508/1123. Print to PDF at 297 × 210 mm with backgrounds enabled.
