AUDIENCE (STEP 1)
Demographics: IUB students, 18–25, all departments.
Lifestyles: classes, exams, group work, phones and social media.
Values: friendship, fairness, being respected and included.
HOW TO RUN IT (STEP 3)
Show the A4 draft to 10 students who have not seen it. Let each look for 30 seconds, then take it away. Ask Q1–Q6; tick ✓ for each correct answer.
The six questions
Q1
What is the main message of this poster?
Expected: Being heard leads to change: speak, listen, act together.
Q2
What is the 2026 theme line written on it?
Expected: “Lived experiences heard: real voices, real change.” (close wording is fine)
Q3
Name one thing it asks you to do.
Expected: Any one: join the conversation, check in on a friend, offer peer support, challenge stigma.
Q4
What does the picture show, from left to right?
Expected: Someone unheard → a bridge of conversation → being included at the table.
Q5
(Leaflet) Where can someone find support?
Expected: Any one: someone you trust, university counselling, 16263, 999.
Q6
Is anything confusing, upsetting, stigmatising or hard to read?
Expected: Write the answer word for word in the comments column.
