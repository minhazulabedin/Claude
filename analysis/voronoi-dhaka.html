    Recommended new sites 3

      Placed by the largest-empty-circle rule — the point inside the city farthest from every existing facility, chosen one after another.

      Method

      1Collect points. Approximate lon/lat of each facility per service type across Dhaka.
      2Tessellate. Every point in the city is assigned to its nearest facility — the Voronoi partition (computed here as a fine distance raster inside the city boundary).
      3Score accessibility. Distance-to-nearest-facility (km) per location gives mean / median / worst access and the share of the city beyond the chosen threshold.
      4Recommend. Greedy farthest-point search proposes new sites where the current gap is largest.
      Data disclaimer. Facility coordinates are approximate, hand-compiled for a methodological case study — not an official register. Distances use an equirectangular projection about latitude 23.78°N (1° lat ≈ 110.6 km, 1° lon ≈ 101.9 km) and are straight-line, not road-network, distances. Treat magnitudes as indicative.
